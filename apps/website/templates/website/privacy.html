Privacy

CyberQuest follows the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. We collect only what a school needs: names, class, results and (for parents who opt in) a contact email or phone number.

  • Data is kept by the school's own CyberQuest installation.
  • Students cannot upload files. Staff uploads are checked for type and size.
  • AI never receives student names or personal details.
  • Parents can unsubscribe from every email.
  • Every login, change and "Login as" is written to a security or audit log.
  • To see or delete a child's data, contact the school or contact us.
