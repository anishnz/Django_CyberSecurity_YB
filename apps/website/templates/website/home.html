What is CyberQuest?

A cyber-safety learning platform for New Zealand schools. Students (Ākonga) learn in short topics, practise with kind feedback, and pass a 20-question exam to move up six levels. Teachers (Kaiako) see simple reports; schools manage everything in one place.

How it works

1. Study (Ako)

Short topics with Kiwi and marae examples – 3 to 7 minutes each.

2. Practise

Instant feedback after every answer. Mistakes help us learn.

3. Prove it

A 20-question exam; pass to earn a badge, a certificate and the next level.
