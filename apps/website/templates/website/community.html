Te Tiriti o Waitangi and our {% L 'community' %}

How CyberQuest is built with, and for, Māori and local communities.

Partnership

Māori Community Liaison advisers and a Cultural Guide (Kaiārahi) are part of the team from the first meeting. They help decide requirements and check content.

Participation

Whānau (families), kura and teachers test the app. Labels use English first with te reo Māori in brackets. Examples come from everyday Kiwi life and the marae.

Protection

Children's data stays with their school, students cannot upload files, AI never receives names, and parents must opt in to messages. Kind feedback – never the word "Fail".

Benefits for the community

  • Safer online habits for tamariki (children)
  • Whānau kept informed by email in plain English
  • Cultural content checked by the Kaiārahi
  • 50% discount for kura and small rural schools
  • A pathway into digital and cyber careers
